Kat Sitwayen

Gid Fòmasyon Konbit 3,5%

Pou chak manm ak chak òganizatè. Li l, pataje l, pratike l an ekip. Disiplin se zam nou.

1. Kiyès nou ye2. Plan an nan 5 minit3. Disiplin san vyolans4. Fòme yon ekip 10 moun5. Kijan pou envite 3 moun6. Kalme yon sitiyasyon7. Konnen dwa ou8. Premye swen9. Sekirite ak telefòn10. Pale ak moun ki pa dakò11. Aksyon nou yo12. Lis tcheke pou yon aksyon

1. Kiyès nou ye

Konbit 3,5% se yon mouvman sitwayen, san vyolans, ki vle yon Leta ki sèvi pèp la. Nou vize 3,5% Ayisyen — anviwon 416,500 moun — òganize nan chak seksyon kominal.

  • Nou pa yon pati politik. Okenn pati pa posede nou.
  • Nou pa gen yon sèl chèf. Pòtpawòl yo chanje chak sis mwa.
  • Nou pibliye chak goud ki antre ak chak goud ki soti.
  • Nou pa janm sèvi ak vyolans.

Yon sèl fraz pou retni: "Nou vle yon Leta ki sèvi pèp la, pa yon Leta ki sèvi kèk moun."

2. Plan an nan 5 minit

Lè w ap eksplike plan an bay yon moun, di senk bagay sa yo:

  1. Lajan Leta a parèt aklè. Chak goud Leta depanse parèt sou yon rejis piblik tout moun ka wè.
  2. Pa gen salè san konkou. Moun ki pa pase egzamen an epi ki pa sou lis la pa touche. Fini ak travayè fantom ak moun yo plase pa relasyon.
  3. Sitwayen òdinè chita nan Leta a. Yon Asanble Sitwayen yo tire osò, tankou yon jiri, kontwole lwa yo ak bidjè a.
  4. Sèvis rapid e menm jan pou tout moun. Chak biwo afiche sa pou w pote, konbyen sa koute ak konbyen tan sa pran.
  5. Sekirite ak fwontyè sou kontwòl. Lame veye fwontyè a 24 è sou 24, lapolis rebati, gwoup ame dezame.

Fini ak: "Tout plan an sou sit la, an kreyòl. Vin wè, epi di sa w panse."

3. Disiplin san vyolans

  1. Pa janm frape, menm si yo frape w. Pwoteje tèt ou, epi kite.
  2. Pa janm pote zam, ni zam blanch, ni wòch, ni baton.
  3. Pa janm boule, kraze, oswa piye anyen.
  4. Pa janm bloke yon lopital, yon anbilans, yon mache, oswa yon wout malad ap sèvi. Pa gen peyi lòk.
  5. Pa janm joure, ni menase, ni moun ki pa dakò avè w.
  6. Pa janm pran lajan nan men gwoup ame, nan men politisyen, oswa pou mouvman an san resi.
  7. Pa bwè alkòl ni pran dwòg pandan yon aksyon.
  8. Si yon moun pote vyolans, kite l, ale yon lòt kote, epi rapòte sa bay responsab ekip ou. Moun ki vyolan pa manm nou ankò.

Poukisa? Kanpay san vyolans yo genyen apeprè de fwa plis pase kanpay vyolan yo, paske plis moun ka antre ladan yo e moun ki anndan sistèm nan pi fasil chanje bò. Vyolans bay moun ki gen pouvwa yon rezon pou kraze nou.

4. Fòme yon ekip 10 moun

  1. Chwazi 9 moun ou konnen e ou fè konfyans: fanmi, vwazen, kanmarad travay, legliz.
  2. Chak moun pran Kat Sitwayen li sou sit la (oswa sou papye ak responsab la).
  3. Chwazi yon responsab ekip pou 6 mwa, ak yon asistan. Apre 6 mwa, yo chanje.
  4. Rankontre chak semèn, 1 èdtan, menm jou, menm lè.

Lòd jou yon reyinyon (60 minit):

MinitKisa
0–10Nouvèl chak moun: kijan nou ye, kiyès ki bezwen èd
10–25Aprann yon chapit nan gid sa a
25–40Planifye aksyon semèn nan (enskri votè, konbit, envite moun)
40–55Pratike: eksplike plan an, kalme yon sitiyasyon
55–60Kiyès ki fè kisa, ki lè; dat pwochen reyinyon an

Lè ekip la rive 10 moun, ede yon manm kòmanse yon nouvo ekip.

5. Kijan pou envite 3 moun

Chwazi twa moun ou konnen. Pale avè yo youn pa youn, pa nan gwo gwoup.

"Ou menm tou ou bouke ak yon Leta ki pa sèvi nou? Gen yon mouvman san vyolans k ap òganize nan tout peyi a. Pa gen chèf, pa gen pati, chak goud parèt aklè. Nou vize 416,500 moun. Èske w ta vin nan yon reyinyon ak mwen semèn pwochèn?"

  • Koute plis pase w pale. Mande: "Kisa ki pi fè w mal nan peyi a?"
  • Pa fòse pèsonn. Yon "non" jodi a ka tounen "wi" demen.
  • Voye lyen envitasyon ou an (sou Kat Sitwayen ou) pa WhatsApp.

6. Kalme yon sitiyasyon

  1. Rete kalm. Respire. Pale dousman, ak yon vwa ba.
  2. Mete distans. Pa kanpe twò pre. Montre men ou louvri.
  3. Koute. Di: "Mwen tande w." Repete sa moun nan di.
  4. Pa diskite nan mitan foul la. Mennen moun nan yon ti kote.
  5. Mete ekip la ansanm. Kenbe youn lòt, pa janm rete pou kont ou.
  6. Si sa vin danjere, kite. Pa gen okenn aksyon ki vo yon lavi.

7. Konnen dwa ou

Konstitisyon 1987 la garanti libète pou sitwayen rasanble san zam e pasifikman, ak libète pou yo eksprime yo. Men kèk règ pratik (sa pa ranplase konsèy yon avoka):

  • Pote kat idantite ou sou ou.
  • Si yon polisye rete w, rete kalm, pa kouri, pa fè rezistans. Mande poukisa yo rete w.
  • Ou gen dwa mande pou w pale ak yon avoka.
  • Pa siyen anyen ou pa konprann.
  • Ou ka filme yon aksyon piblik san w pa bloke polisye a. Pa filme figi timoun.
  • Ekri tout sa ki pase (dat, lè, kote, non oswa nimewo badj) epi voye l bay responsab ekip ou.
  • Mouvman an gen yon lis avoka volontè; mande responsab ou nimewo a.

8. Premye swen

  • Sekirite anvan: pa antre nan danje pou ede yon moun. Rele èd.
  • San k ap koule: peze fò sou blesi a ak yon twal pwòp, san rete, jiskaske èd rive.
  • Moun ki pa reponn men ki respire: mete l sou kote li (pozisyon sekirite), tèt li yon ti jan anba.
  • Moun ki pa respire: rele èd touswit; si w konnen kijan, kòmanse masaj kè.
  • Gaz lakrimojèn: ale nan van, pa fwote je w, lave ak anpil dlo pwòp.
  • Chalè: lonbraj, dlo, twal mouye.
  • Chak ekip pote dlo ak yon bwat premye swen nan chak aksyon.

9. Sekirite ak telefòn

  • Pa kenbe lis manm sou telefòn ou oswa sou papye ki ka pèdi. Sit la kenbe yo an sekirite.
  • Sèvi ak mesaj ki gen chifraj (pa egzanp Signal oswa WhatsApp), mete mesaj ki efase tèt yo pou gwoup òganizasyon yo.
  • Mete yon modpas oswa yon kòd sou telefòn ou.
  • Pa pibliye kote ak lè yon aksyon twò bonè.
  • Pa poste foto moun ki pa dakò, ni foto minè.
  • Si yon moun ou pa konnen mande lis manm, adrès oswa lajan, refize epi rapòte sa.

10. Pale ak moun ki pa dakò

  • Respè anvan tout bagay. Pa janm joure.
  • Mande kisa ki enkyete moun nan, epi koute.
  • Montre kote plan an reponn enkyetid sa a (sit la gen tout pati yo).
  • Di laverite: si w pa konnen, di "M ap chèche repons lan."
  • Ou pa bezwen genyen chak diskisyon. Kite pòt la louvri.

11. Aksyon nou yo

EtapAksyon
1Enskri pou vote, epi ede lòt moun enskri
2Pran Kat Sitwayen an
3Jou konbit: netwaye, repare, plante, ansanm
4Rankont piblik: mande chak kandida siyen Angajman Sitwayen an
5Jou koulè: tout moun mete menm koulè, a menm lè
6Rara ak mizik ak mesaj la
7Veye eleksyon yo nan biwo vòt
8Jou nasyonal 3,5%: tout moun aji ansanm nan pwòp komin yo

Sa nou pa fè: baryè, kawotchou boule, peyi lòk, fèmen mache, atake byen moun.

12. Lis tcheke pou yon aksyon

Anvan:

  • Kote a an sekirite (pa nan zòn gwoup ame)
  • Yon responsab sekirite, yon responsab premye swen, yon moun k ap filme
  • Dlo, bwat premye swen, nimewo avoka
  • Tout moun konnen règ disiplin yo ak kote pou yo ale si gen danje

Pandan:

  • Rete ansanm an ekip
  • Mete moun ki vyolan deyò
  • Kite si sa vin danjere

Apre:

  • Tout moun rive lakay yo? Rele youn lòt
  • Ekri rapò a: konbyen moun, sa ki byen mache, sa pou chanje, nenpòt ensidan
  • Netwaye kote a

Konbit 3,5% Training Guide

For every member and every organizer. Read it, share it, practise it as a team. Discipline is our weapon.

1. Who we are2. The plan in 5 minutes3. Nonviolent discipline4. Forming a team of 105. How to invite 3 people6. Calming a situation7. Know your rights8. First aid9. Phone safety10. Talking with people who disagree11. Our actions12. Action checklist

1. Who we are

Konbit 3,5% is a nonviolent citizens' movement for a State that serves the people. We aim for 3.5% of Haitians — about 416,500 people — organized in every communal section.

  • We are not a political party. No party owns us.
  • We have no single leader. Spokespeople rotate every six months.
  • We publish every gourde received and spent.
  • We never use violence.

One sentence to remember: "We want a State that serves the people, not a State that serves a few."

2. The plan in 5 minutes

  1. Public money in the open. Every gourde the State spends appears on a public ledger anyone can see.
  2. No salary without an exam. Nobody is paid unless they passed the exam and are on the list. No more ghost workers or jobs for friends.
  3. Ordinary citizens inside the State. A Citizens' Assembly drawn by lot, like a jury, checks laws and the budget.
  4. Fast, equal service. Every office posts what to bring, what it costs and how long it takes.
  5. Security and a controlled border. The army watches the border 24 hours a day, the police are rebuilt, armed groups are disarmed.

End with: "The whole plan is on the website, in Kreyòl. Come and see, and tell us what you think."

3. Nonviolent discipline

  1. Never strike, even if you are struck. Protect yourself and leave.
  2. Never carry a weapon, a blade, stones or sticks.
  3. Never burn, break or loot anything.
  4. Never block a hospital, an ambulance, a market or a road the sick use. No peyi lòk.
  5. Never insult or threaten anyone, including people who disagree.
  6. Never take money from armed groups or politicians, or for the movement without a receipt.
  7. No alcohol or drugs during an action.
  8. If someone brings violence, move away, go elsewhere and report it to your team leader. Violent people are no longer members.

Why? Nonviolent campaigns have won about twice as often as violent ones, because many more people can join and people inside the system change sides more easily. Violence gives those in power a reason to crush us.

4. Forming a team of 10

  1. Choose 9 people you know and trust: family, neighbours, co-workers, church.
  2. Each gets a Citizen Card on the website (or on paper with the leader).
  3. Choose a team leader for 6 months and an assistant. After 6 months, they rotate.
  4. Meet every week, for 1 hour, same day, same time.

Meeting agenda (60 minutes):

MinutesWhat
0–10News from each person: how we are, who needs help
10–25Learn one chapter of this guide
25–40Plan the week's action (voter registration, konbit, inviting people)
40–55Practise: explaining the plan, calming a situation
55–60Who does what, by when; date of next meeting

When the team reaches 10, help one member start a new team.

5. How to invite 3 people

Choose three people you know. Talk to them one at a time, not in a big group.

"Are you tired of a State that does not serve us? There is a nonviolent movement organizing across the country. No boss, no party, every gourde in the open. We aim for 416,500 people. Would you come to a meeting with me next week?"

  • Listen more than you talk. Ask: "What hurts you most in this country?"
  • Never push. A "no" today can be a "yes" tomorrow.
  • Send your invitation link (on your Citizen Card) by WhatsApp.

6. Calming a situation

  1. Stay calm. Breathe. Speak slowly and low.
  2. Keep distance. Do not stand too close. Show open hands.
  3. Listen. Say: "I hear you." Repeat what the person said.
  4. Do not argue in the middle of the crowd. Take the person aside.
  5. Keep the team together. Hold on to each other; never stay alone.
  6. If it becomes dangerous, leave. No action is worth a life.

7. Know your rights

The 1987 Constitution guarantees citizens the freedom to assemble peacefully without weapons and to express themselves. Some practical rules (this does not replace a lawyer's advice):

  • Carry your ID card.
  • If police stop you, stay calm, do not run, do not resist. Ask why you are stopped.
  • You have the right to ask for a lawyer.
  • Do not sign anything you do not understand.
  • You may film a public action without blocking the officer. Do not film children's faces.
  • Write down everything (date, time, place, name or badge number) and send it to your team leader.
  • The movement keeps a list of volunteer lawyers; ask your leader for the number.

8. First aid

  • Safety first: do not walk into danger to help. Call for help.
  • Bleeding: press hard on the wound with a clean cloth, without stopping, until help arrives.
  • Unresponsive but breathing: lay the person on their side (recovery position), head slightly down.
  • Not breathing: call for help at once; if you know how, start chest compressions.
  • Tear gas: move into the wind, do not rub your eyes, rinse with plenty of clean water.
  • Heat: shade, water, wet cloth.
  • Every team brings water and a first-aid kit to every action.

9. Phone safety

  • Do not keep member lists on your phone or on paper that can be lost. The website keeps them safe.
  • Use encrypted messaging (for example Signal or WhatsApp) and turn on disappearing messages in organizing groups.
  • Put a password or code on your phone.
  • Do not announce the place and time of an action too early.
  • Do not post photos of people who did not agree, or of children.
  • If someone you do not know asks for member lists, addresses or money, refuse and report it.

10. Talking with people who disagree

  • Respect first. Never insult.
  • Ask what worries them, and listen.
  • Show where the plan answers that worry (the website has every Part).
  • Tell the truth: if you do not know, say "I will find the answer."
  • You do not need to win every argument. Leave the door open.

11. Our actions

StepAction
1Register to vote, and help others register
2Get the Citizen Card
3Konbit days: clean, repair, plant, together
4Town halls: ask every candidate to sign the Citizens' Pledge
5Colour days: everyone wears the same colour, at the same hour
6Rara and music with the message
7Watch the elections at polling stations
8National 3.5% day: everyone acts together in their own commune

What we do not do: barricades, burning tires, peyi lòk, closing markets, attacking property.

12. Action checklist

Before:

  • The place is safe (not in an armed-group area)
  • A safety lead, a first-aid lead, and someone filming
  • Water, first-aid kit, lawyer's number
  • Everyone knows the discipline rules and where to go if there is danger

During:

  • Stay together as a team
  • Remove anyone violent
  • Leave if it becomes dangerous

After:

  • Did everyone get home? Call each other
  • Write the report: how many people, what went well, what to change, any incident
  • Clean the place
